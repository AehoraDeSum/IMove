Routes

Choose B1 Marmaray, M2 and M6 stations and add transfers. You can edit walking paths and waypoints on the map.

Nearby transfers at Levent and Yenikapı are connected automatically. For distant boarding points, add a walking segment first. Line geometry is approximate map data, not live service information. The M2 Şişhane–Taksim path is also incomplete.

In waypoint mode, tap the map to reshape the segment by hand; the walking calculation passes through these waypoints. Apple Maps is used online, with FOSSGIS Valhalla as a fallback.
